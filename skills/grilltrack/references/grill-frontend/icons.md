# Icons

- Evaluate the icon system in the accepted layout, at real sizes and states.
- Make form language, stroke, fill, metaphor, or container treatment materially
  distinct.
- Avoid a six-glyph exception; every agent-generated round still has exactly
  five candidates.
- Wire a precise user-requested hybrid directly only after preview and
  confirmation.
- Verify alignment, recognizability, contrast, and accessible labels.
