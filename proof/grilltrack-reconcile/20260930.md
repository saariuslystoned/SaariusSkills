eight shared decisions and a 35-event prefix; a paused continuation with four
additional decisions and 70 events; a closed archived base and a direct
successor with four additional decisions and 33 events; twenty shared closed
archive pairs. Apply preserves all source files byte-for-byte, all sixteen
decision IDs, and every original archive. The composed projection invalidates
old verification/review and refuses closure. Normal CLI progress survives a
repeated apply.

changed target state, contradictory shared decisions, malformed events,
broken common prefix, changed historical archives, unrelated commits,
symlinked receipt, and missing retained source. Failure injection before
publication, after ledger publication, and after event publication restores
canonical bytes. A simulated process exit after ledger publication blocks
ordinary commands, including `new`, until exact approved retry recovers.

produce sixteen decision IDs with `applied=false`; hashes of all twenty-two
canonical/archive files match before and after. Those pins are reproduction
fixtures, not approval of the authors' final heads. No product apply, plugin
installation, or merge was performed. Raw product evidence stays local.
